\textbf{How the three parts work together.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Part I --- Theory \& Derivations.} Every proof and worked numeric simulation. Reproduce each boxed derivation from memory.
\item \textbf{Part II --- Problem Bank.} MCQs, multi-step problems and \emph{trap questions} per topic. This is where ``conceptually basic but hard to answer'' questions live: the ones where the obvious answer is wrong.
\item \textbf{Part III --- Mock Quiz.} A full mixed paper under time pressure, with an answer key. Sit it cold --- mixed topics are far harder than topic-by-topic drilling, and that is what the real quiz feels like.
\end{itemize}
