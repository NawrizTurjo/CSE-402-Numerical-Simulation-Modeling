1. We were asked to implement the Power Iteration Method to compute the dominant eigenvalue and its corresponding eigenvector.

A 4×4 matrix was provided, along with the algorithmic steps for implementing the Power Iteration Method.

After obtaining the result from our implementation, we were instructed to verify it using NumPy's eigenvalue computation.

Important notes:

Before returning the eigenvector from our implementation, it must be normalized. Otherwise, the result will not match NumPy's output.

NumPy's eigenvalue routine may return an eigenvector with the opposite sign. Since both v and −v are valid eigenvectors for the same eigenvalue, this sign difference is expected and does not indicate an error. Our custom implementation may therefore produce the correct eigenvector even if its sign differs from NumPy's output.
